About this resource

Developed for ASIANLAN 115

Developer
Santosh Desai developed this site for ASIANLAN 115, Section 003, at the University of Michigan.
Instructional purpose
Students review one lecture at a time, then take a quiz on that same topic. The notes stay with the lesson, and each quiz draws only on the unit just studied.
Approach to teaching
The sequence starts with the Devanagari script and concrete vocabulary, then grammar and verb forms, then use in context: shopping, daily routines, and telling the time. Students first see a pattern with examples, pictures, and a Devanagari keyboard. They then practice it by choosing, matching, sorting, arranging words, and writing in Hindi. A separate page lets students type with the on-screen Devanagari keyboard and hear the words. Feedback explains the form, including gender, number, case, and the choice among तू, तुम, and आप. Hindi words have a speaker. Female or male, and a slower or faster speed, can be chosen on every page.
Data and privacy
The quiz does not ask for a UMID. A name is optional. Answers stay in the browser tab and are not uploaded. A result file is created only when a student exports or prints, and it stays on their device unless they share it. A speaker downloads the chosen open Piper Hindi voice once from Hugging Face, about 64 MB, and a small pronunciation library. Only the Hindi text is spoken, and it is not uploaded. The type-and-listen page uses that same voice. What a student types stays in the browser and is not uploaded.

Quiz settings

Privacy. Your name is optional, and this quiz does not ask for a UMID. If you enter a name, it is kept with your answers only in this browser tab so you can refresh and continue. Nothing is sent to the instructor, the developer, or a server. Closing the tab clears the saved quiz. Export and print create a file on your device that includes your name, if you entered one, and your answers. That file is shared only if you choose to share it.
